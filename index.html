About Reach Together

Reach Together was created to solve the common problem of solo travel among colleagues. Our platform aims to make journeys safer, more economical, and more enjoyable by connecting people from the same organization who are traveling on similar routes.

Whether you're concerned about safety during late-night travel, looking to share transportation costs, or simply want company during a long journey, Reach Together helps you find the perfect travel companion from your own trusted organization.
